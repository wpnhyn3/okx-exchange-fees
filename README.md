## What "global" actually means in 2026

A global crypto exchange is, in theory, a platform that serves users across many countries with a broadly consistent product. In practice, almost every major exchange now runs a patchwork of regional entities, each with its own product list, fiat rails, and regulatory ceiling. The global brand on the homepage is the marketing layer; what you actually get is the local version.

That matters for two reasons. First, the product you came for — usually derivatives, sometimes a specific altcoin listing — might not be available where you are. Second, the fiat on-ramp and off-ramp that look clean in a review might not work in your country at all. Reading "global exchange" and assuming you'll get the full product is the most common way new users end up disappointed.

So the real checklist when comparing global exchanges is shorter than most reviews make it sound:

- Does it serve your country at all?
- Does it offer the products you actually want to use (spot, perps, options, margin, staking)?
- What are the real maker/taker fees at your trading volume, not the headline number?
- Can you get money in and out through a route that isn't going to cost you 3% in card spread?
- Is there a verifiable reserve report, or just a "trust us" claim?

Everything else — UI polish, number of listed coins, app ratings — is secondary. Most of the top global exchanges are competent on the basics. The differences show up in the corners.

## Funding, withdrawals, and the rails that actually matter

The fee schedule gets the attention, but the on-ramp and off-ramp are where most users actually lose money. OKX supports different fiat rails by region:

- **US:** Free ACH deposits and withdrawals ($20–$20,000), domestic wire ($1M+ limits), instant debit-card deposits in eligible states. ACH in / ACH out is the cleanest loop.
- **EEA:** Free EUR deposits and withdrawals by bank transfer, plus cards and local payment methods. USDT is not available — USDC and USDG are the MiCA-compliant stablecoins.
- **UK:** More constrained, especially around derivatives, CeFi, and P2P.
- **Rest of world:** Crypto deposits, P2P with 100+ payment methods, cards, and local third-party channels depending on jurisdiction.

Crypto withdrawals are network-dependent. Choosing TRC-20 for USDT instead of ERC-20 typically saves meaningful amounts on every cash-out. Always confirm the destination wallet supports the same network before sending — wrong-chain transfers are one of the most common and most avoidable mistakes in crypto.

KYC is mandatory and layered. Initial identity verification (government ID + selfie) opens the core product. Additional documentation — proof of address, source of funds — can be requested later, and during review, OKX can pause login, withdrawals, deposits, and trading simultaneously. The official review window is 24 hours, but complex cases take longer. Complete full verification before funding if you plan to move larger amounts.
